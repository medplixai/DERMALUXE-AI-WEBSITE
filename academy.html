FAQ

Questions trainees ask us

Who can join DermaLuxe Academy?

Cosmetologists, beauty therapists, salon and spa professionals, nursing and paramedical staff, aesthetic clinic staff and beauty entrepreneurs. Freshers with a genuine interest in skin and hair aesthetics can also apply — message us and our team will guide you.

Is the training really hands-on?

Yes. Every module is practised on live clients inside our working clinic, on USFDA-approved machines, under the direct supervision of our MD dermatologist. Batches are limited to 10 so every trainee gets machine time.

1 month or 2 months — which should I choose?

The 1-month course gives you the core procedures and a certificate. The 2-month advanced course adds complex cases, combination protocols, more live-client hours and an internship — best if you want to be placed quickly or run your own clinic.

When does the batch start and how do I reserve a seat?

Admissions are open from 10 September 2026 and Batch 1 starts on 20 October 2026. Message us on WhatsApp — seats are confirmed in the order of admission, and only 10 are available.

Do you provide jobs after the course?

Yes. We also provide jobs at our own locations — DermaLuxe by Medicare and the Medicare Skin & Hair Clinics family across Andhra Pradesh. Successful trainees get priority in our hiring.

What are the fees?

Fees depend on the course and duration you choose. Message us on WhatsApp and our team will share the fee structure and payment options.

In which language is the training?

Telugu and English, with the clinical terms in English exactly as you will use them at work.

I am from outside Eluru — can I attend?

Yes. Trainees come from across West Godavari and Andhra Pradesh. Our team can guide you on nearby stay options when you enquire.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes — after a practical assessment you receive the DermaLuxe Academy certificate. The 2-month course also includes an internship letter.
