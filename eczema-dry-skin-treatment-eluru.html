Frequently asked questions

Is eczema curable?

Eczema is a tendency of the skin, so it can flare again, but with correct treatment and barrier care most people stay largely flare-free and many children outgrow it.

Are steroid creams dangerous?

Used in the right strength, on the right area, for the right duration under a dermatologist, they are safe and very effective. Problems come from wrong self-use for months. We also use steroid-free options.

Is eczema contagious?

No. It cannot spread to others by touch.

Which soap should I use?

A fragrance-free, soap-free cleanser and lukewarm (not hot) water, followed by moisturiser within 3 minutes. Your doctor will recommend specific products.
