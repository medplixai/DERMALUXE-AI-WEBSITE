Frequently asked questions

Are hives dangerous?

Most hives are harmless though uncomfortable. Swelling of the lips, tongue or throat, or breathing difficulty, is an emergency — go to a hospital immediately.

How do I find what I am allergic to?

Through a detailed history, and where needed patch testing or blood tests. Many chronic hives have no external allergen and are managed with medication rather than avoidance.

Can I stop antihistamines once I feel better?

Chronic urticaria needs continuous control for a period before tapering. Stopping early causes rebound. Your dermatologist will set the schedule.

Is it safe to use fairness or herbal creams with allergy-prone skin?

Many such creams contain steroids, mercury or fragrances that trigger reactions. Use only products your dermatologist approves.
