Why acne needs a dermatologist, not a facial

Acne has different causes — excess oil, hormones, bacteria, PCOS, steroids or the wrong cosmetics. Treating the wrong cause is why over-the-counter creams and parlour clean-ups fail and why marks keep coming back. Your doctor first identifies the grade and type of acne, then plans a medical protocol that clears active breakouts and prevents new scars. మొటిమలకు కారణం ఒక్కొక్కరికి వేరు — ఆయిల్, హార్మోన్లు, PCOS లేదా తప్పు కాస్మెటిక్స్. మా MD చర్మ వైద్యులు ముందు కారణం గుర్తించి, ఆ తర్వాత చికిత్స ప్లాన్ చేస్తారు.

What results to expect

Active acne usually settles within 6–12 weeks of medical treatment; scar procedures such as MNRF or CO2 laser are done in 3–5 sessions spaced 4–6 weeks apart, with visible smoothening after each session. Frequently asked questions

Can acne be cured permanently?

Acne can be brought under complete control and kept clear with the right medical treatment and maintenance plan. Hormonal or PCOS-related acne may need longer-term care, which your dermatologist will explain.

Do acne scars go away on their own?

Red and brown marks fade with treatment over a few months. Pitted or depressed scars do not fill on their own — they need procedures like MNRF, fractional CO2 laser or subcision.

Is acne treatment painful? Is there downtime?

Peels feel like mild tingling with no downtime. MNRF and laser sessions are done with numbing cream; expect mild redness for 1–3 days.

Which doctor will treat my acne?

All acne and scar treatments at DermaLuxe are planned and performed by qualified MD dermatologists.
