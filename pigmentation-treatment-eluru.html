Not all dark spots are the same

Melasma is hormonal and sun-driven and sits deep in the skin; sun tan and freckles are superficial; post-inflammatory marks follow acne or injury; some patches are early signs of other skin conditions. Each needs a different approach — the wrong cream or an aggressive parlour treatment can actually make pigmentation darker. Frequently asked questions

Can melasma be removed permanently?

Melasma can be controlled and lightened significantly, but it is a chronic condition that can return with sun exposure or hormonal changes. A maintenance plan and daily sunscreen keep it under control.

Is PICO laser safe for Indian skin?

Yes. PICO laser delivers ultra-short pulses that break pigment without heating the surrounding skin, which makes it one of the safest laser options for darker skin tones when done by a dermatologist.

How many sessions will I need?

Most patients need 4–6 sessions spaced 3–4 weeks apart, depending on the type and depth of pigmentation. Your doctor will confirm after examining your skin.

Does pigmentation treatment have downtime?

Peels and PICO sessions have little to no downtime — mild redness for a few hours and some flaking with peels. You can return to work the same day.
