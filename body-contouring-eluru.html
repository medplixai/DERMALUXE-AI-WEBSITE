What body contouring can and cannot do

Non-surgical contouring reduces localised fat pockets and tightens skin in specific areas; it is for shaping, not for large weight loss. If overall weight is the issue, our medical weight-loss programme comes first and contouring finishes the result. Frequently asked questions

Is non-surgical fat reduction safe?

Yes when done under medical supervision with approved devices. There are no cuts, anaesthesia or recovery time.

How much fat can I lose?

Contouring reduces localised pockets by a visible amount per session; it is not a substitute for weight loss. We give a realistic estimate at the consultation.

Will the skin become loose after fat reduction?

We combine fat reduction with skin-tightening treatments where needed so the contour stays smooth.

Can I do this after C-section?

Yes, once healed (usually 6 months after delivery) and cleared by your doctor.
