Frequently asked questions

Is skin treatment for men different from women?

Men's skin is thicker and oilier and shaves daily, so products and settings are adjusted. The treatments are the same medical-grade options, planned for men's needs.

Can hair loss in men be stopped?

Yes, early male-pattern hair loss responds well to medical treatment and PRP/GFC. Advanced baldness is treated with hair transplant.

Will laser hair removal change my beard?

Only the areas you choose are treated — for example, neck and cheek lines for a sharp beard shape. The beard itself is untouched.

How long do treatments take?

Most sessions take 20–45 minutes with no downtime, so they fit a working day.
