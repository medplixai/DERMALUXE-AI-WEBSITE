Frequently asked questions

Can I use my own cream on my child's rash?

Please do not. Adult creams, especially steroid combinations, can harm children's thin skin. A dermatologist will prescribe the right product and strength.

Is eczema in babies curable?

Most babies improve greatly with a barrier-care routine and outgrow eczema by school age. Flares are controlled quickly with safe treatment.

My teenager has acne — is it too early to treat?

No. Early treatment prevents scars. Teen acne responds very well to dermatologist care.

Are warts in children serious?

No, but they spread by touch. Gentle treatment removes them and prevents spreading to siblings.
