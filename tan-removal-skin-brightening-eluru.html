Brightening vs bleaching

Your natural skin tone is fixed by genetics; medical brightening brings you back to it by removing tan, dead cells and surface pigment. 'Fairness' creams that promise a lighter tone often contain steroids or mercury and cause thinning, acne and rebound pigmentation. Frequently asked questions

Can my skin become fairer than my natural colour?

No treatment can safely change your genetic skin tone. Medical brightening removes tan and pigment to reveal your natural, even tone — which is often lighter than the tanned skin you are used to seeing.

Is there downtime after brightening peels?

Superficial peels have no downtime; you may see light flaking for 2–3 days. Carbon laser has none.

Are these treatments safe for dark Indian skin?

Yes. The peels and lasers we use are chosen and set specifically for Indian skin tones by a dermatologist.

How do I keep the glow after treatment?

Sunscreen every 3 hours outdoors, a simple prescribed routine and a maintenance session every 1–2 months.
