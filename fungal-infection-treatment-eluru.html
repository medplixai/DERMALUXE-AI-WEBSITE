Why your fungal infection keeps coming back

Combination creams containing steroids (often sold as 'itch creams') thin the skin and let the fungus spread wider and deeper — the infection looks better for a week, then returns worse. Sharing towels, tight synthetic clothes, sweat and humidity keep it going. Frequently asked questions

Why do I get ringworm again and again?

Most often because of steroid-mixed creams, incomplete treatment, re-infection from family members or clothes, and sweat. A dermatologist's plan addresses all of these.

Are the antifungal tablets safe?

Yes when prescribed in the right dose and duration by a dermatologist, with liver tests if the course is long. They are far safer than months of steroid creams.

Can I use the cream from the medical shop?

Please avoid creams with unknown or combination ingredients. Many contain steroids that worsen fungal infections over time.

How do I stop it spreading in my family?

Treat everyone affected at the same time, do not share towels or clothes, wash clothes in hot water and dry in the sun, and wear loose cotton.
