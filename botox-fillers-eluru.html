Botox: smooth expression lines in 15 minutes

Frequently asked questions

Will Botox make my face look frozen?

Not when done correctly. Our doctors use conservative, precisely placed doses so you keep natural expressions while lines soften.

Are fillers permanent? Can they be removed?

Hyaluronic acid fillers are not permanent — they last 9–18 months and are gradually absorbed. If ever needed, they can be dissolved by the doctor.

Is there any downtime after Botox or fillers?

Botox has no downtime. Fillers may cause mild swelling or a small bruise for 1–3 days, which makeup can cover. Avoid heavy exercise and massage of the area for 24 hours.

At what age should I start Botox?

There is no fixed age. Botox is used when expression lines start staying on the skin at rest — for many people that is the late 20s to 30s. A doctor consultation decides whether you need it at all.
