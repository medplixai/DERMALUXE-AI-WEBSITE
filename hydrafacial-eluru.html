Frequently asked questions

How long does a Hydrafacial take and does it hurt?

About 45 minutes. It is completely painless — most patients describe it as a cool, relaxing massage.

When will I see results and how long do they last?

The glow is visible immediately after the session and lasts about 4–6 weeks. A monthly Hydrafacial keeps the skin consistently clear and hydrated.

Can I get a Hydrafacial before my wedding or an event?

Yes — it is the ideal pre-event treatment. Book it 2–3 days before the event for the best glow, with no risk of redness or peeling.

Is Hydrafacial safe for acne-prone or sensitive skin?

Yes. Because it uses gentle suction and mild serums instead of scrubbing, it suits sensitive and acne-prone skin. Your dermatologist adjusts the intensity and serums accordingly.
