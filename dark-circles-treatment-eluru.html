What type of dark circles do you have?

Brown or black circles are usually pigmentation (genetic, sun, rubbing from allergies). Bluish or purple circles come from thin skin over blood vessels, often with lack of sleep or anaemia. A shadow under a hollow is volume loss (tear trough), common after 30. Most people have a mix, which is why one cream rarely works. నల్లగా ఉంటే పిగ్మెంటేషన్, నీలంగా ఉంటే పలుచటి చర్మం, గుంటలా ఉంటే వాల్యూమ్ లాస్. మీ రకం బట్టి చికిత్స మారుతుంది.

Frequently asked questions

Can dark circles be removed permanently?

Pigmentation-type circles improve significantly and can be kept away with maintenance and sun protection. Hollow-type circles are corrected with fillers that last 9–12 months. Genetic circles can be lightened but may need periodic care.

Are under-eye fillers safe?

Yes, when done by a qualified doctor using USFDA-approved hyaluronic acid fillers with the right technique for the thin under-eye skin. At DermaLuxe only doctors perform injectables.

Is laser safe near the eyes?

PICO laser is used on the under-eye skin with protective eye shields by a trained dermatologist. It is safe and widely used for pigmented dark circles.

Will home remedies like cucumber or potato work?

They may cool and soothe puffiness for a few hours but do not treat pigmentation, thin skin or hollows.
