Frequently asked questions

Who should get a skin check?

Anyone with a changing or new mole, a non-healing sore, a family history of skin cancer, fair skin, many moles, or long-term outdoor sun exposure such as farmers and construction workers.

Is skin cancer common in Indian skin?

It is less common than in fair skin but does occur, especially on sun-exposed areas and in people with long outdoor exposure. Early detection matters just as much.

Does the biopsy hurt?

It is done under local anaesthesia and takes a few minutes. You go home the same day with a small dressing.

How often should I be screened?

Once a year if you have risk factors, and immediately for any changing lesion. Your dermatologist will advise based on your findings.
