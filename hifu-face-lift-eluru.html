How HIFU works

Focused ultrasound energy is delivered precisely to the SMAS layer — the same layer a surgeon tightens in a face lift — and to the deep dermis. This triggers new collagen and gradual contraction, so the skin lifts from within. The surface is untouched, which is why there is no wound or recovery time. అల్ట్రాసౌండ్ ఎనర్జీ చర్మం లోపలి పొరను టైట్ చేస్తుంది, సర్జరీలో టైట్ చేసే అదే పొర. పైన గాయం ఉండదు, డౌన్‌టైమ్ లేదు.

Frequently asked questions

Who is HIFU for?

Adults from their 30s onward with mild to moderate sagging who want lifting without surgery. Severe laxity may need a surgical opinion, which we will tell you honestly.

Is HIFU painful?

You feel heat and brief pricking sensations during the pulses. Numbing cream and adjusted settings keep it comfortable for most patients.

Is HIFU safe for Indian skin?

Yes. Because it works below the surface without heating the top layer, it is safe for all skin tones when performed by a trained dermatologist on a USFDA-approved device.

How long do results last?

12–18 months, after which a maintenance session keeps the lift. Results also depend on age, skin quality and lifestyle.
