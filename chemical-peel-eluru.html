A chemical peel uses a controlled medical-grade acid to remove the damaged top layers of skin and trigger fresh, even-toned skin underneath. Chosen and applied by a dermatologist, it is one of the safest, most effective treatments for acne, tan, pigmentation and dull skin — very different from the harsh 'peels' offered at parlours.

Types of peels we use

  • Salicylic peel — oily, acne-prone skin, blackheads & open pores
  • Glycolic & lactic peels — dull skin, tan, fine lines and glow
  • Mandelic & arginine peels — sensitive skin and gentle brightening
  • Yellow (retinol) peel — pigmentation, melasma & early ageing
  • TCA peel & TCA CROSS — deeper marks and acne scars
  • Body peels — underarms, neck, back acne, knuckles & elbows

What a peel session is like

After cleansing, the peel solution is applied for a few minutes — you feel mild tingling or warmth, then it is neutralised. The session takes 20–30 minutes. Superficial peels have no downtime; medium-depth peels cause light flaking for 3–5 days. What results to expect

Skin looks brighter and smoother after the first session. For acne, tan and pigmentation, a course of 4–6 peels spaced 2–4 weeks apart gives lasting improvement, often combined with prescription creams or laser for deeper concerns. Frequently asked questions

Are chemical peels safe for Indian / dark skin?

Yes, when the right peel and strength are chosen by a dermatologist. Incorrect peels can cause dark patches on Indian skin, which is why we do not recommend parlour peels.

Does a chemical peel hurt?

No. You feel mild tingling or warmth for a few minutes. Deeper peels may sting briefly but are well tolerated.

Will my skin peel visibly?

Superficial peels cause little or no visible peeling. Medium peels cause light flaking for 3–5 days, similar to mild sunburn recovery.

How many peel sessions do I need?

Most concerns need 4–6 sessions spaced 2–4 weeks apart. Your dermatologist will confirm the number after examining your skin.
