Frequently asked questions

Can nail fungus be cured?

Yes, with the correct antifungal for the full duration and, in some cases, nail lacquers or removal of the damaged part. Home remedies rarely reach the fungus under the nail.

Is ingrown toenail surgery painful?

The procedure is done under local anaesthesia and is painless. You walk out the same day with mild soreness for a day or two.

Will the toenail grow back normally after surgery?

Only the ingrowing edge is removed and treated so it does not regrow; the rest of the nail stays and looks normal.

Why are my nails brittle?

Frequent water and detergent exposure, nail polish removers, thyroid problems and nutritional deficiencies are common causes. A dermatologist can identify and correct the cause.
