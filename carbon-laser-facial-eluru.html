How it works

Medical carbon lotion is applied and sinks into the pores. A Q-switched or PICO laser then passes over the skin: the carbon absorbs the light and is blasted away, taking impurities with it, while the laser energy gently heats the dermis to stimulate collagen and shrink pores. Frequently asked questions

Is carbon laser facial safe for Indian skin?

Yes. The settings are gentle and the treatment is widely used on darker skin tones when performed by a dermatologist.

How is it different from Hydrafacial?

Hydrafacial deep-cleans and hydrates with suction and serums; carbon laser uses laser energy to refine pores, control oil and stimulate collagen. Many patients alternate the two.

Is there any pain?

You hear popping sounds and feel light warmth. No numbing is needed.

How soon before an event should I do it?

1–3 days before for the best glow.
