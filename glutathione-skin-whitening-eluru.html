Frequently asked questions

Is glutathione injection safe?

When given by a doctor in the correct dose with quality products, it is generally well tolerated. It should not be taken from beauty parlours or unlicensed providers. We screen every patient first.

How many sessions are needed?

Usually 6–10 weekly sessions, then monthly maintenance depending on your response and goals.

Will it make me permanently fair?

No. It brightens and evens your skin within your natural range. Results need maintenance and sun protection.

Are there side effects?

Mild and uncommon — occasional nausea or a rash. Our doctors monitor every session; it is avoided in pregnancy and certain medical conditions.
