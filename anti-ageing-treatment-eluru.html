How skin ages — and what we can do

Expression lines come from repeated muscle movement (Botox relaxes them). Volume loss in cheeks and under the eyes creates hollows and shadows (fillers restore them). Collagen loss causes sagging and crepey texture (HIFU, MNRF, threads and PRP rebuild it). Sun damage causes pigmentation and dullness (peels, lasers and skincare correct it). Frequently asked questions

What age should I start anti-ageing treatment?

Prevention with sunscreen and retinoids can start in the late 20s. Treatments like Botox or HIFU are chosen when lines or laxity appear, usually 30s onward. There is no fixed age; it depends on your skin.

Will I look unnatural?

Not with our approach. Doctors use conservative doses and plan for subtle, balanced results.

Which single treatment gives the most benefit?

It depends on your main concern: lines → Botox, hollows → fillers, sagging → HIFU or threads, texture → MNRF/PRP. Most patients benefit from a combination over time.

Are these treatments safe?

Yes when performed by qualified doctors with USFDA-approved products and devices, as at DermaLuxe.
