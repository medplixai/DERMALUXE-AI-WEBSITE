Frequently asked questions

Will removing a mole leave a scar?

With RF or CO2 laser done by a dermatologist, the mark is usually tiny and fades over a few months with proper aftercare. Cutting at home or in a parlour is what causes bad scars.

Do warts come back after removal?

Warts are viral, so a few may recur or new ones appear if the virus is still active. Complete removal plus follow-up reduces this, and recurrences are treated early.

Is mole removal safe? Can it become cancer?

Removal by a dermatologist after a dermatoscopic check is safe. Any suspicious mole is sent for testing rather than simply burned, which is the safe practice.

How long does the procedure take?

Most removals take 10–20 minutes. You can go home immediately and return to work the same day.
