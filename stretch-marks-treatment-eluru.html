Red vs white stretch marks

Fresh stretch marks are red or purple and respond fastest to treatment. Older marks turn white or silvery as collagen is lost — they need collagen-rebuilding treatments like fractional CO2 laser and MNRF. Frequently asked questions

Can stretch marks be removed completely?

They can be made significantly lighter, flatter and much less visible, but no treatment removes them 100%. Early red marks respond best.

Is stretch mark laser safe after delivery?

Yes, once your doctor confirms you are ready (usually 3–6 months after delivery and not while breastfeeding, depending on the treatment). We plan it safely for you.

Is the treatment painful?

Numbing cream is applied before laser and MNRF sessions; you feel warmth and mild pricking. It is well tolerated.

Do creams from the market work?

Over-the-counter creams can slightly improve very early red marks but do not remove established marks. Prescription retinoids help more, and lasers give the real results.
