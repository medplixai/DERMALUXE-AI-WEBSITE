How a thread lift works

Fine PDO (polydioxanone) threads with tiny cogs are placed beneath the skin with a thin cannula and gently pulled to reposition sagging tissue. The threads hold the lift while your body forms collagen around them; they dissolve safely over 6–8 months. The procedure is done under local anaesthesia by a doctor. Frequently asked questions

Is a thread lift safe?

Yes when done by a trained doctor with quality PDO threads in a clinical setting. The threads are the same material used in surgical sutures and dissolve completely.

How is it different from HIFU?

HIFU tightens gradually with no downtime; a thread lift gives an immediate mechanical lift with a few days of recovery. Many patients combine both for the best result.

Will I look 'pulled' or unnatural?

No. Our doctors plan thread placement for a subtle, natural lift matched to your face, never an overdone look.

At what age can I get a thread lift?

Typically from the mid-30s onward when early sagging appears. The consultation confirms whether it is the right option for you.
