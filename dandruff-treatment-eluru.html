Why dandruff keeps coming back

Most dandruff is caused by a yeast (Malassezia) that feeds on scalp oil and triggers inflammation. Oily scalp, stress, humidity, infrequent washing, hard water and some hair products make it worse. Supermarket shampoos suppress it for a few days; without treating the inflammation and the fungal load, the flakes and itching return — and chronic dandruff weakens the roots and increases hair fall. చుండ్రుకు కారణం తలపై ఉండే ఒక ఫంగస్. షాంపూతో కొన్ని రోజులు తగ్గినా, మూలకారణం చికిత్స చేయకపోతే మళ్ళీ వస్తుంది, జుట్టు రాలడం పెరుగుతుంది.

Frequently asked questions

Is dandruff the reason for my hair fall?

Often, yes. Chronic dandruff inflames the scalp and weakens hair roots. Treating the scalp first usually reduces hair fall within a few weeks.

Do I need to see a doctor for dandruff?

If dandruff has lasted more than a month, itches, has red patches or thick scales, or is causing hair fall, a dermatologist should examine it — it may be seborrheic dermatitis, psoriasis or a fungal infection that needs prescription treatment.

Can oiling the hair cure dandruff?

No. Oil feeds the yeast that causes dandruff and usually makes it worse. Use oil only as advised by your dermatologist.

How often should I wash my hair?

For an oily, dandruff-prone scalp, 3–4 times a week with the medicated shampoo prescribed for you. Your doctor will set the routine based on your scalp.
