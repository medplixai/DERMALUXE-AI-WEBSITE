Why PICO laser

PICO laser fires ultra-short pulses (trillionths of a second) that break ink without heating the surrounding skin. That means fewer sessions than Q-switched lasers, less risk of scarring or pigmentation, and the ability to treat black, blue, green and red inks. Removal by acids, salt or burning causes permanent scars — never try it. What results to expect

Amateur and black-ink tattoos clear in 4–6 sessions; dense professional colour tattoos may need 8–10. Sessions are spaced 6–8 weeks apart to let the body clear the ink. Frequently asked questions

Does tattoo removal leave a scar?

With PICO laser done correctly, scarring is rare. Skin may look slightly lighter or darker temporarily and evens out with sunscreen and aftercare.

Is it painful?

It feels like a rubber band snapping on the skin. Numbing cream and cooling make it very tolerable.

Can a tattoo be removed completely?

Most tattoos fade to near-invisible; some very dense or certain-colour inks leave a faint ghost. We show you realistic expectations at the first session.

Is PICO laser safe for dark skin?

Yes. Its ultra-short pulses spare the surrounding pigment, which is why it is the preferred laser for Indian skin.
