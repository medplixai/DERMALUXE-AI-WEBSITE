Frequently asked questions

Will my visit be confidential?

Completely. Records are private and nothing is discussed with family or anyone else without your consent.

Can I consult online first?

Yes. A teleconsultation lets you discuss symptoms privately; tests and procedures are then arranged at the clinic if needed.

Do I need tests?

Often yes, because different infections look similar. Tests are simple and results guide the exact treatment.

Should my partner be treated?

For many infections, yes, to prevent re-infection. Your doctor will guide you discreetly.
