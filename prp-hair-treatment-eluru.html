How PRP & GFC work

PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) and GFC (Growth Factor Concentrate) are prepared from a small sample of your own blood. Frequently asked questions

How many PRP/GFC sessions are needed?

Typically 4–6 monthly sessions, then maintenance every few months. Your dermatologist will personalise the plan after examining your scalp.

Is GFC better than PRP?

GFC is a more refined, higher-concentration preparation with consistent results and minimal discomfort. Our doctor will recommend what suits your hair loss stage.

When will I see results?

Reduced hair fall is usually noticed in 4–8 weeks; visible density improvement from about 3 months onwards.
