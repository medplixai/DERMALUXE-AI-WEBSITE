Why pores look large

Pores enlarge when excess oil and dead cells stretch them, when collagen around them weakens with age and sun, and after repeated acne. Scrubbing and pore strips damage the skin barrier and make oil production worse. The fix is to control oil, clear the pores and rebuild collagen around them. అధిక ఆయిల్, మృత కణాలు, వయసుతో కొల్లాజెన్ తగ్గడం వల్ల పోర్స్ పెద్దగా కనిపిస్తాయి. స్క్రబ్ చేయడం వల్ల ఇంకా పెరుగుతాయి.

Frequently asked questions

Can open pores be closed permanently?

Pores are natural openings and cannot be closed, but they can be made much smaller and less visible with MNRF, carbon laser and the right skincare. Results last with a maintenance routine.

Is carbon laser safe for Indian skin?

Yes. Carbon laser facial uses a gentle laser on a carbon layer and is safe for darker skin tones when done by a dermatologist. No downtime.

Will treating oily skin make it dry?

No. The aim is balance, not stripping. Your dermatologist chooses actives that control oil while protecting the skin barrier.

How often do I need treatment?

Usually 3–4 sessions a month apart, then a maintenance session every 2–3 months along with your home routine.
