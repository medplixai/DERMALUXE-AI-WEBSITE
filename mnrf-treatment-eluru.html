How MNRF is different from ordinary microneedling

Ordinary dermaroller microneedling only pricks the skin. MNRF adds controlled radiofrequency heat at a set depth through insulated needles, which remodels scar tissue and tightens collagen far more effectively, with less surface damage and lower risk of pigmentation — a key advantage for darker skin. సాధారణ మైక్రోనీడ్లింగ్ కంటే MNRF లో RF హీట్ లోపలికి వెళ్ళి మచ్చలు, పోర్స్‌ను బాగా తగ్గిస్తుంది. ఇండియన్ స్కిన్‌కు సురక్షితం.

What MNRF treats

  • Acne scars: rolling, boxcar and mixed scars
  • Open pores and rough, uneven texture
  • Fine lines and early skin laxity on face and neck
  • Stretch marks on tummy, thighs and arms
  • Surgical and injury scars
  • Combined with PRP or subcision for deeper scars

What results to expect

Skin looks smoother after the first session; scars and pores improve progressively with 3–5 sessions spaced 4–6 weeks apart, and collagen keeps building for months afterwards. Numbing cream is applied for 30–40 minutes; the session takes 20–30 minutes; expect redness for 1–3 days and tiny scabs that fall off in a week. మొదటి సెషన్ నుంచే స్మూత్‌నెస్, 3–5 సెషన్లలో మచ్చలు, పోర్స్ బాగా తగ్గుతాయి. 1–3 రోజులు ఎరుపు, వారంలో పూర్తిగా మామూలు.

Frequently asked questions

Is MNRF painful?

Numbing cream makes it comfortable; you feel warmth and pressure. Most patients rate it as mild.

How many MNRF sessions do acne scars need?

Usually 3–5, depending on scar depth. Deep ice-pick scars may also need TCA CROSS or subcision, which we combine in the plan.

Will MNRF cause dark marks on Indian skin?

The insulated needles protect the surface, making MNRF one of the safest scar treatments for darker skin. Sunscreen after treatment is important.

When can I apply makeup or go out?

Light makeup and normal activity from day 2–3. Avoid direct sun and workouts for 48 hours.
