Common causes in women

Telogen effluvium after delivery, illness, surgery or crash dieting; iron, vitamin D and B12 deficiency; thyroid disorders; PCOS with hormonal hair thinning; female-pattern hair loss with age; tight hairstyles and heat damage. Frequently asked questions

Is hair fall after delivery normal?

Yes, it is common 2–4 months after delivery and usually settles by 6–12 months. If it is severe, lasts longer or the parting keeps widening, a dermatologist should check for deficiencies or thyroid issues.

Can I take hair treatment while breastfeeding?

Several treatments, including deficiency correction and PRP, are safe during breastfeeding. Your dermatologist will choose accordingly.

Does PCOS hair loss improve?

Yes, with combined care: hormonal management with your gynaecologist, targeted scalp treatment and PRP/GFC for regrowth.

Will my hair become as thick as before?

Most women regain significant density when the cause is treated early. Long-standing pattern thinning improves but needs maintenance.
