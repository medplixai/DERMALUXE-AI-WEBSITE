How beard and eyebrow transplant works

Follicles are taken from the back of the scalp (which never balds) and implanted into the beard or brow area with fine punches, following the natural direction and angle of growth. It is a day-care procedure under local anaesthesia. The implanted hair is permanent and can be trimmed and styled like the rest. తల వెనుక నుంచి హెయిర్ ఫాలికల్స్ తీసి గడ్డం లేదా కనుబొమ్మల్లో సహజ దిశలో నాటుతారు. లోకల్ అనస్థీషియా, అదే రోజు ఇంటికి.

What results to expect

Transplanted hairs shed in the first few weeks and regrow permanently from the third month, reaching full result by 8–12 months. Beard transplants typically use 800–2,500 grafts and eyebrows 150–350 grafts depending on the design. Frequently asked questions

Is a beard transplant permanent?

Yes. The follicles come from the permanent zone of the scalp and keep growing for life.

Will it look natural?

Natural results depend on angle, direction and density planning. Our surgeons design each beard and brow to match your face and existing hair.

Does it hurt?

The procedure is done under local anaesthesia; you feel only the initial numbing. Mild soreness for a day or two afterwards.

Can I shave or trim the transplanted beard?

Yes, after healing (about 2–3 weeks). It behaves exactly like your natural beard.
